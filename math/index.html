1. सूचना संग्रह और उपयोग (Information Collection & Use)

स्थानीय भंडारण (Local Storage): एप्लिकेशन केवल आपके डिवाइस के लोकल स्टोरेज (Hive डेटाबेस) का उपयोग आपके द्वारा हल किए गए मॉक टेस्ट, स्कोर, समय, गलत उत्तरों की समीक्षा और बुकमार्क किए गए प्रश्नों को सहेजने के लिए करता है। यह पूरा डेटा आपके मोबाइल फोन पर ही सुरक्षित रहता है और ऐप को अनइंस्टॉल करने पर स्वतः डिलीट हो जाता है।

कोई संवेदनशील अनुमति नहीं (No Sensitive Permissions): एप्लिकेशन को आपके डिवाइस की लोकेशन, कैमरा, माइक्रोफ़ोन, संपर्क (Contacts) या स्टोरेज जैसी किसी भी संवेदनशील अनुमति की आवश्यकता नहीं होती है।

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 02, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the software application UP TGT Mathematics (UP TGT गणित) ("Application") for mobile devices. The Application is designed for offline competitive exam preparation and mock test series for UP TGT Mathematics aspirants.

Key Takeaway: The Application runs 100% offline and does NOT collect or transmit any of your personal data to remote servers.

1. Information Collection and Use

Offline First: The Application operates completely offline. We do not collect, store, track, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to external servers or third parties.

Local On-Device Storage: The Application uses local storage (Hive Database) exclusively on your device to store test attempt scores, performance metrics, bookmarks, and user preferences. All data remains stored locally on your device and is permanently removed when you uninstall the Application.

No Sensitive Permissions: The Application does not request high-risk or sensitive runtime permissions such as GPS Location, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, or Phone Storage.

The Application does not currently use any third-party SDKs, ad networks, or tracking services that collect data used to identify you.

These Services are intended for competitive exam candidates and do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
