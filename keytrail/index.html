KeyTrail - Typing Tutor

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 11 October 2026
Developer: AMAN KUMAR
Android package: com.keytrail.tutor

Your practice data stays strictly on your device. KeyTrail is a free, 100% offline touch typing tutor. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no remote server connections.

1. Zero Remote Data Collection

KeyTrail does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No Internet Permission: The app does not declare or use the android.permission.INTERNET permission. It cannot send or receive data over the network.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: No third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or Crashlytics) are included.
  • No Advertising: The app contains zero advertisements or ad networks.
  • No User Accounts: No account creation, login, email address, or phone number is required.

2. Local On-Device Storage

All application progress is stored privately in the sandboxed local storage on your device:

  • Typing Session History: Net WPM, accuracy, session duration, and mistake counts are stored in a local SQLite database (Room).
  • Curriculum Progress: Completed lessons, active streaks, and high scores are tracked locally.
  • User Preferences: Typing mode (English, Hindi Mangal, Hindi Kruti Dev), font size, haptic feedback, and sound toggles are saved in SharedPreferences.

This data never leaves your device. You can reset your practice history at any time inside the app ("Settings > Reset progress"). Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data.

3. Device Permissions

Permission Classification Purpose
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal Provides optional haptic feedback on typing errors during practice. Can be disabled in Settings.

4. Educational & Independent Use

KeyTrail is an independent educational training aid. Practice benchmarks and exam profiles (such as SSC, Railway, or Stenographer presets) are provided solely for syllabus alignment and informational practice. KeyTrail is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representative of any government body or recruitment agency.

5. Children's Privacy

KeyTrail is suitable for users of all ages, including children under 13. Because the app does not collect any personal data and operates entirely offline, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy standards.
