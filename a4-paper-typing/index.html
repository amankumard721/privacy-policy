A4 Paper Typing

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 11 October 2026
Developer: AMAN KUMAR
Android package: com.a4sizepaper.typing.texttopdf

Your documents stay on your device. A4 Paper Typing is a free, offline document and PDF editor. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics or automatic document uploads.

1. Information used on your device

The app processes the text you type, document titles and formatting, selected images and signatures, generated PDFs, and app preferences to provide editing, saving, preview and export features. These files and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not receive them from the app.

The app does not request Internet permission and does not include advertising or analytics services. It does not automatically send your documents, usage activity or device identifiers to us.

2. Images and permissions

  • Selected images: the system photo picker gives the app access to images you select. The app creates a private resized copy for your document and removes image metadata from that copy.
  • Notifications: optional notification permission is used to show PDF export progress and cancellation controls.
  • Background export: a foreground service may complete a PDF export you start. It is used for local processing.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, or your entire photo library. Permission choices can be managed in Android settings.

3. Saving, sharing and printing

Files leave the app's private storage when you choose Save as, Share or Print. The destination you select may be another app, a cloud storage provider or a printer. That destination handles its copy under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your document merely because you use these features.

4. Storage and deletion

You can delete documents inside the app. Unused private image copies may be cleaned up at a later startup after a 24-hour grace period. The latest private PDF for each document is retained; earlier PDF versions may be cleaned up after seven days.

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes its private local files. Copies you save, share or print outside the app are separate and must be deleted at their destination. Save important PDFs outside the app before uninstalling.

The app disables Android automatic backup for its private app data. Local files are protected by Android's app storage controls; access also depends on the security of your device.

5. Support messages

If you email amankunar.tech@gmail.com, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Email is handled through our email provider. Avoid sending sensitive documents unless needed for support.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and related follow-up. You may contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

6. This policy page

This web page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it is separate from using the offline Android app. GitHub may process technical request information to serve the page, as explained in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new revision date. The current app also includes a privacy notice in its settings.

For privacy questions or requests, contact AMAN KUMAR at amankunar.tech@gmail.com.
