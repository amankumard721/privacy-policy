Android package: com.a4sizepaper.typing.texttopdf

Document editing and PDF processing happen on your device. No account is required. Advertising and scanning services can communicate with their providers and process technical information. This policy explains that distinction. Features and advertising availability depend on the app version and configuration.

1. Documents and information on your device

The app uses your typed text, document titles, formatting, selected images and signatures, imported Word and spreadsheet files, PDFs, scanned pages and preferences to provide its tools. The app stores working documents and copies in private device storage. Editing, supported file conversion and PDF export can work offline.

We do not operate a document-upload server or receive these files automatically. The app does not include document text, titles, filenames, photos or PDF content in its advertising requests.

2. Advertising and technical information

Advertising-enabled builds use Google AdMob. Meta Audience Network (Facebook) can serve ads through AdMob mediation when that network is configured. Development builds use Google demo ad units; demo ads do not generate publisher revenue, but SDK technical-data processing can still occur.

Ad services may collect and share an IP address and its approximate location, advertising or app/device identifiers, information about app and ad interactions, and performance or diagnostic information. These are used to deliver and measure ads, support analytics and prevent fraud. Data practices depend on the provider, available identifiers, configuration and applicable privacy choices.

AdMob uses Google's privacy services to determine whether ads can be requested. Where a consent message is required, it is presented before ad requests are allowed. The app requests Meta Limited Data Use before ad SDK initialization; that setting does not replace your privacy choices or mean that no information is collected.

Provider information: Google Privacy Policy, Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and Meta Privacy Policy.

3. Your advertising privacy choices

Where required, open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app to review or change the choices offered by Google's User Messaging Platform. Available choices depend on your region and the provider's message. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads; limited or non-personalized ads may still be available.

You can also manage or delete the advertising ID using Android's advertising/privacy settings, where supported. If privacy setup or an ad fails, document tools remain available. You do not need to watch an ad to write, scan, import, export or save a PDF.

4. PDF scanning

Scanning uses Google Play services and ML Kit Document Scanner for camera/gallery capture, page adjustment and PDF creation. ML Kit processes scan images and results on the device; these inputs and outputs are not sent to Google's servers by ML Kit. The app does not automatically upload scanned pages.

Google services may download or update scanner components and contact Google for service maintenance. ML Kit sends performance and utilization metrics to Google. These technical activities are separate from the document content. See ML Kit Terms and Privacy and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions and selected files

  • Photos and files: Android's pickers give access to the files or photos you choose. For editor images, the app creates a resized private copy and removes image metadata from that copy. This is not a claim that every imported document or scanner PDF has all metadata removed.
  • Camera: the scanner capture interface is provided by Google Play services. A4 Paper Typing does not request camera permission directly.
  • Notifications and background processing: optional notifications and a foreground service show progress and cancellation controls for PDF exports you start.
  • Internet and network status: advertising and privacy services use the network. Scanner components may also need an initial download through Google Play services.
  • Advertising identifiers: advertising SDKs declare advertising ID and related Android ad-service permissions. Applicable Android settings and privacy choices control availability.

The app does not request contacts, microphone, Android location permission or broad access to device storage. This does not prevent advertising providers from estimating approximate location from an IP address. Permission controls are available in Android settings.

6. Saving, sharing and printing

When you choose Save as, Share or Print, your document copy goes to the destination you select, such as another app, cloud storage or a printer. That destination handles its copy under its own privacy policy. Support attachments are sent only if you choose to email them. Technical ad and scanner data described above can be transferred separately from these document actions.

7. Storage, retention and deletion

You can delete documents and saved PDFs inside the app. Imported and scanned PDF copies stay in the private PDF library until deleted or app data is cleared. Temporary file-backed scanner output is removed after a successful durable import.

Unreferenced editor images become eligible for cleanup after a 24-hour grace period at a later app startup. Cleanup can be postponed when references cannot be safely checked. The latest private export for each document is retained; older export copies may be cleaned up after seven days. These are cleanup rules, not a guarantee that removing an image from the editor immediately deletes its file.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes private app files. Copies saved, shared, printed or emailed outside the app must be managed at their destination. Save important files outside the app before uninstalling.

Deleting local app data does not delete records already held by advertising providers, Google services, email providers or other recipients. Their retention and deletion controls are described in their privacy policies.

8. Security

The app disables Android automatic backup for its private app data and uses Android's private-storage controls. The document editor restricts remote network content. Protection also depends on your device security and on any destination you choose for a file. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Support and privacy requests

If you email AMAKUNAR.IN@GMAIL.COM, your email address, message and attachments you provide are handled through our email provider to answer your request. Avoid sending sensitive documents unless necessary. Correspondence is retained as needed for the request, related follow-up and applicable obligations.

You may use the same address to ask privacy questions or request deletion of support correspondence we control. For data held by an advertising or platform provider, use that provider's privacy controls or request process. We do not have an app account or server copy of your documents to retrieve.

10. This policy page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not embed ad tags or analytics scripts. GitHub may process technical request information when serving the page, separately from the Android app. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

11. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its revision date when app features or data practices change. A privacy notice is also available in the app's settings.
