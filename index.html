1. Introduction

Welcome to JHARKHAND GK IN HINDI. We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing an offline educational quiz and practice platform for competitive examinations such as JPSC Prelims, JSSC CGL, and other Jharkhand state competitive exams.

2. Information Collection and Use

Zero Personal Data Collection

The JHARKHAND GK IN HINDI app functions 100% Offline.

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers to any external server.
  • No user account registration or login is required to use this App.

Local Storage (On-Device Data)

  • Your quiz statistics, progress tracking, test scores, and bookmarked questions are stored strictly on your device in a local encrypted database (Hive DB).
  • This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server.

3. Device Permissions

The App does not request access to sensitive device permissions, including:

  • Camera or Microphone
  • GPS Location Data
  • Device Contacts or Call Logs
  • SMS or Financial Data

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party tracking software, analytics SDKs, or data-broker services.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our App is an educational utility suitable for students and aspirants of all ages. We do not collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted outside your mobile device, your privacy is safeguarded by your device's native operating system security features.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Effective Date".
